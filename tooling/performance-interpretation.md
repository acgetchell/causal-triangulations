## Interpretation

These timings cover the CDT release-signal workload in `benches/ci_performance_suite.rs` after its integration and physics tests pass.
They measure construction, validation, local moves, and simulation operations; they do not establish ergodicity, thermalization, or continuum behavior.

Compare results only after reviewing host, toolchain, dependency, source, and harness provenance. Added or missing workloads remain visible.
Marginal Criterion confidence intervals are not confidence intervals for a speed ratio or a significance test.
The September 2026 tooling migration starts a new evidence series; old reports and native release assets are not inputs to this series.
